1. Agreement to Purchase.

The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, the property described in Exhibit A of this document, subject to the terms and conditions herein stated. All fixtures and built-in appliances are included unless specifically excluded in writing.

2. Earnest Money Deposit.

Buyer shall deposit the sum of $25,000 with the Escrow Agent within three (3) business days of the Effective Date. This deposit shall be held in a non-interest-bearing account and applied toward the purchase price at closing. In the event of default, the earnest money shall be handled according to state law.

3. Inspections and Contingencies.

The Buyer’s obligation to close this transaction is contingent upon the satisfactory completion of a professional home inspection, pest inspection, and radon testing within fourteen (14) days. Seller agrees to provide reasonable access to the property for these inspections.

4. Closing and Possession.

Closing shall take place on or before the Transfer Date specified above. Possession shall be delivered to the Buyer immediately following the funding of the transaction and recording of the deed, free of all tenants and personal property not included in the sale.

5. Professional Ethics.

Both parties acknowledge that PrimeSquare acts as the facilitating platform and is not liable for structural defects or undisclosed encumbrances. All data transmitted through this portal is encrypted and legally binding under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN).
